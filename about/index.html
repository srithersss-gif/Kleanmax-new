Our Story

Founded over 13 years ago, Kleanmax began with a simple mission: to deliver reliable, high-quality professional cleaning services to businesses that needed a partner they could truly trust. Starting with a small team and a handful of corporate clients in Chennai, we have grown into one of the city's most respected commercial and industrial cleaning companies.

Today, we serve over 1,000 B2B clients across all major business zones in Chennai — from IT parks on OMR to heavy manufacturing plants in Sriperumbudur and Oragadam. Our growth has been built entirely on client referrals and long-term partnerships, which speaks to the consistency and quality of our work.

We operate under the philosophy of "Happy Mind Service" — believing that a genuinely clean and well-maintained workspace directly contributes to the productivity, morale, and wellbeing of everyone who uses it.
